About us

Built by students, for students.

CampusConnect started as a simple idea: campuses lose thousands of items every semester — ID cards, wallets, chargers, water bottles — and most never make it back to their owners because there's no central place to look. We built CampusConnect to fix that.

🎯

Our Mission

Make it effortless for students to report, discover, and recover lost belongings across campus.

🧠

Smart Matching

We automatically compare newly found items against lost reports so likely matches surface instantly.

🔒

Safe & Verified

Every account is tied to a verified student ID, department, and year — keeping the community trustworthy.

How CampusConnect helps

Whether you've misplaced your ID card between lectures or found a stray umbrella outside the canteen, CampusConnect gives every item a home page: photos, description, location, and a direct line to the person who can help. Our lightweight matching engine flags potential matches by category and item name so nothing sits unclaimed for long.

The platform is maintained as an ongoing student project, with the Django-powered admin panel giving campus staff full oversight of reports, users, and resolved cases.
